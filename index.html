End-to-End Voice Architecture

How J.A.R.V.I.S. achieves instantaneous voice dialogue through modular asynchronous streaming stages.

01

Silero VAD Buffer

Continuous 16kHz PCM ring buffer in Rust. Analyzes speech probability every 30ms, activating on speech and stopping after 350ms silence.

02

Faster-Whisper STT

Streams raw audio chunks into optimized CTranslate2 Whisper model. Generates hypothesis tokens with interim punctuation repair.

03

Hybrid Intent Router

Local heuristic analyzer determines whether query requires local execution (Ollama) or complex reasoning (Gemini Flash).

04

Chunked Neural TTS

Synthesizes sentence fragments immediately upon first token arrival, streaming audio buffers back to speaker before LLM finishes completion.
